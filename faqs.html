⛏ Mythica FAQs

Make sure you read the rules first!

1How do I make money?

Easily! Earn $ through fishing, mining, voting, auction house, or your very own playershops with a pwarp! You can also browse the /shop to see prices on things you can buy/sell.

2Can I use mods?

Keeping in mind the server rules, any mod that makes gameplay UNFAIR is not allowed.
Things we do currently allow: Fullbright, Xaeros Minimap (with caves turned off), Xaeros World Map (personal dynmap) and Litematica.

3 Is Mythica p2w (Pay to win)?

Nope! We do have donor ranks for purchase, but everything they aquire you can earn through just playing on the server!
We have multiple leveling and ranking systems set up so that everyone can unlock perks!

4Does Mythica have a Discord and do I have to join it?

We do! You can find the link on our homepage and in game by using the /discord command. While you do not have to join our discord, it is highly reccomended.
Discord hosts information on events, server changes, polls and if you need assistance.

5How do I claim and protect my land?

Here at Mythica we use GriefPrevention. You can claimland using a golden shovel! Watch the video below.

6Help I'm in jail! Why was I jailed?

Here at Mythica we have a really good anti-cheat system. If you have found yourself in jail, you have broken one or more of our rules.
NOTE: Sometimes there are instances where it has jailed you for some reason like super bad lag/ping issues on your end, it is very sensitive. Please contact us through Discord and we will be able to help.
Default jail time is 1 Hour and will increase with each attempt at breaking rules.

7How can I appeal a punishment? Ban, jail, mute, etc.

You will need to have a Discord account, appeals form is through dyno. Click Here to access the form.

8Why can't I connect to the server?

Double check your settings, the IP is Mythica.Online port: 25565 for bedrock. You can troubleshoot how to join Here .

9How often do we reset the world?

To put it simply, we don't! We are focused on keeping your builds and hard earned progress for everyone to see.
The Resource world does reset however, to continue obtaining materials.

10I have found a bug, should I report it?

YES, immediately! If you find something that is not working as intended, please let a staff member know.
There will be rewards in reporting bugs, glicthes and exploits.

11Is PVP on and will I lose my stuff if I die?

PVP is off by default, you can have mutual combat of both players use the command /pvp
KeepInventory is also enabled, so when you die you will keep everything! So pvp away.
