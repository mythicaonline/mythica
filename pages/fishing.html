💰 Money & Entropy

Two of the most important resources in PyroFishing.

🪙

Money

Sell fish through /fish shop. Tournaments, deliveries and certain augments can increase your profits.


Fishing income can reach up to $10,000 every 8 hours, after which the income resets.

🔮

Entropy

Earn entropy from catching fish, gutting fish, completing deliveries and participating in tournaments.


Check your balance with /fish stats or /fish menu.

🔪 Gutting Fish

Destroy fish to convert them into valuable Entropy.

Use /fish gut or the Gutting Station in /fish menu.


Gutting destroys the fish but rewards Entropy. The rewards can be improved with:


  • 🔪 Precision Cutting Augment
  • 📈 Better Gutting Skill

🧩 Fishing Augments

Custom rod enchants that change how you fish.

Lv 10 🌊 Hot Spot Chance to catch 2× fish.
Lv 12 ⛈️ Call of the Storm Double fish while raining.
Lv 12 🍗 Saturate Chance to refill hunger.
Lv 12 📚 Sage More fishing XP.
Lv 16 🌎 Biome Disruption Catch fish from any biome.
Lv 22 🔪 Precision Cutting More Entropy from gutting.
Lv 25 🪱 Crab Bait Higher crab spawn rate.
Lv 25 🧠 Intellect More fishing XP.
Lv 28 👁️ Perception More Entropy from catching.
Lv 35 🏆 Trophy Better Scales profit.
Lv 35 ☀️ Solar Rage More money when selling.
Lv 45 🎣 Master Fisherman Increased higher-tier fish chance.
⚗️ Crafting: Augments are crafted using a water-filled cauldron. Put the required materials inside and hit the cauldron with your fishing rod.
