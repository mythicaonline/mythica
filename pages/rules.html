⛏ Mythica Server Rules

Play fair. Have fun. Respect the community.

1No Griefing

Griefing and Stealing are not allowed within claims, Not claimed areas are fair game. That still doesn't give permission to destroy an area just because you can. No Lava Casting!

2No Cheating

Hacks, unfair modifications, exploits, x-ray, and other tools that provide an unfair advantage are prohibited. Inluding: AutoClicking, macros, scripts, seed cracking etc. Our anti-cheat is pretty good.

3No Advertising

This server does not allow any kind of server advertising of other MC servers or services via in game or discord. If you get DM'd from another player advertising another server, report it!

4Do not be rude or harass players

Do not be rude to other players or harass them in any way. It's a MC server and we don't want any drama. This is a community. It's a place where people come to relax and have a good time with others. This includes but not limited to: Insults, bullying, stalking, trolling, discrimination, offensive language, DOXing or giving out personal information of others.

5No 18+ Inappropriate or NSFW Content, Skins, Nicknames etc.

Do NOT post or build any inappropriate / NSFW (not safe for work) content in any way, shape or form. This rule is to ensure that the players do not come in contact with inappropriate content. We want a safe place for all ages and inappropriate content does not belong on a MC server. We get many kids from all ages. Staff has final say in what is and isn't inappropriate.

6Don't promote drugs or drug use

This includes but not limited to: Talking or discussing drugs, drug use or other drug related subjects, Renaming items to resemble IRL drugs. Pretending to sell drugs with in-game items (named or unnamed)

7No excessive swearing

This server allows swearing, just not too much of it. If you want to swear, keep in mind rule 4.

8Anything deemed to cause excessive lag will be removed or stopped

This rule contains man-made farms or buildings that may cause excessive lag. This includes, but not limited to: Farms with a massive amount (hundreds or more) of entities Builds that use a very large amount of pistons, especially if they all fire at once and/or very rapidly Giant auto-sorters Redstone Machines (repeating is absolutely not allowed, lag-machines etc.)

9Punishment evasions

Trying to evade a punishment or bypass a punishment is NOT allowed. Once the punishment has been given, the player in question must wait until this punishment is over. The punishment will be extended or upscaled if needed. It will also be given to any alts. This include but not limited to: Alt's (evading a punishment of any kind with an alternative MC account) Using any kind of hacks / exploits to get out of a punishment (i.e getting out of jail) Using a VPN to bypass IP bans and using signs to chat while muted. All appeals can be done on discord, feel free to contact a staff member if you are not able to access it.

10Use Common Sense

Not every situation can be covered by a rule. Use common sense and help keep the server fun for everyone.

⚠️ Important: Staff reserve the right to take action against behavior that seriously harms the server or its community. Punishments may range from warnings to temporary or permanent bans depending on the situation.
