⛏️ PyroMining - Guide

PyroMining adds new drops, bosses, currency, artifacts, and upgrades to everyday mining.

Important: Natural, unplaced ores are what count toward all PyroMining drops and progression.

🍃 Quick Start

  1. Mine natural ores. Silk Touch is fine — drops still roll. Placed ores don't count.
  2. Sell basics at /mine shop such as Fluxes, Gemstones and identified loot.
  3. Open the UI with /mine menu for Shop, Artifacts, Vessels, Journal and more.
  4. Build a Refinery using a dropper facing upward with an oak fence on top.
  5. Level up by mining. XP requirements scale by 1.125× each level.
Check your stats with /mine stats.

🦴 Fossils

Fossils are obtained by identifying artifacts.

Identification Time
60 minutes
Parts Required
8

Collect all 8 parts of the same creature:

Skull · Arm · Spine · Rib · Tail · Wing · Leg · Foot

Once you have a complete set, use a Refinery to craft a Vessel.

Fossils are the only path to Vessels.

⚗️ Vessels

Vessels provide custom pickaxe enchantments.

  1. Craft a Vessel in the Refinery from 8 matching Fossils.
  2. Apply the Vessel to your pickaxe by dragging it onto the pickaxe.
  3. This begins Vessel Progression and marks your pickaxe.
  4. Progress through Thralls and eventually a Vessel Boss.
  5. Defeat the boss using the correct Requium order.
  6. The Vessel enchantment is then applied.
⚠️ Only one Vessel can be progressed at a time on a pickaxe.

Vessels can be removed through /mine vessels for a Zeta fee. This returns Vessel items for levels and progress on that pickaxe.

Each Thrall kill grants:

  • Requium Progress: +5.25 to +10.75
  • Anger: +1.00 to +5.00

Anger reveals the required Requium orb slot-by-slot as it fills.

Vessel Boss

Boss Base Stats
  • 150 HP
  • 2 Damage
  • +50 HP per Vessel level
  • +2 Damage per Vessel level
  • Maximum HP: 2000

The Vessel Boss spawns while mining during progression.

To defeat it, place the correct three Requium orbs in /mine vessels.

⚠️ Requium order matters. A failed order check causes the boss to despawn.

Requium Orbs

  • Found in higher-tier artifacts.
  • Shattered Requium Orbs can be combined: 4 shards → 1 random Requium.
  • Set orbs for Slots 1–3 in /mine vessels.

🧿 Oracles

Oracles are end-game side bosses.

Summon Block: EMERALD_ORE

You need Charged Oracleite. Refine Mysterious Dust at level 20+ to obtain Oracleite.

Mine 35 Diamond Ore while holding Oracleite in your off-hand to charge it.

Phases

Phase Required Level HP Damage
Phase 1 40 1100 14
Phase 2 45 1600 18
Phase 3 50 2000 28
⏱️ Oracle summons have a 30-minute cooldown.

Oracle phases can teleport, gain movement buffs, and have unique drop tables.

Drops include high-tier artifacts, runes needed for later phases, Zeta Tears, and other advanced items.
